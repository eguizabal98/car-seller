# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Building a high-end second-hand car marketplace requires shifting from a "listing site" mentality to a "digital showroom" experience. To provide that "excellent experience," the focus must be on transparency, high-quality media, and low-friction communication.

## 1. Core User Experience (Buyer Side)
The goal is to provide enough detail that a user feels they have virtually inspected the car before even talking to a human.

### A. High-Fidelity Product Pages
- **Media Suite**: Support for 4K photo galleries, 360° interior/exterior viewers, and embedded cold-start/walk-around videos.
- **Interactive Hotspots**: Clickable icons on car photos that reveal details about specific features or minor cosmetic wear (transparency builds trust).
- **The "Digital Logbook"**: A dedicated section for service history, number of previous owners, and downloadable inspection reports (PDF).
- **Specs at a Glance**: A clean, icon-based grid for mileage, fuel type, transmission, and horsepower.

### B. Advanced Discovery & Filtering
- **Smart Search**: Filter by lifestyle (e.g., "Family-friendly," "Track-ready") rather than just make/model.
- **Comparison Tool**: A side-by-side view to compare technical specs and pricing of up to three vehicles.
- **Saved Searches & Alerts**: Push notifications or emails when a car matching specific criteria is listed.

### C. Self-Service Tools
- **Financing Calculator**: Real-time monthly payment estimates based on adjustable down payments and loan terms.
- **Trade-in Valuation**: A simple form where users input their current car details to get an instant (estimated) trade-in value.

## 2. Communication & Conversion
To bridge the gap between browsing and buying:
- **Integrated Live Chat**: A floating chat widget that connects directly to the sales team.
- **Visit Coordinator**: A custom-built automated calendar booking system where users can select a time slot for a test drive or a video call walk-through.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: A "Quick Chat" button for users who prefer mobile-first communication.

## 3. Admin Panel (The Command Center)
This is where your team manages the "machine" behind the website.

### A. Inventory Management (VDP Manager)
- **Bulk Uploader**: Easily upload high-res media.
- **Status Toggles**: Mark cars as "Available," "Reserved," "Sold," or "Coming Soon."
- **Price Management**: History log of price changes and "Featured" status toggles for homepage promotion.

### B. Lead & Chat Management
- **Lead Dashboard**: View all incoming inquiries, categorized by "Hot," "Warm," or "Follow-up Required."
- **Appointment Calendar**: A centralized view of all scheduled visits and test drives for the sales team.
- **CRM Integration**: Store user contact info, car preferences, and interaction history.

### C. Analytics & Reporting
- **Vehicle Performance**: See which cars are getting the most views, clicks, and chat inquiries.
- **Conversion Rates**: Track the journey from "Viewed Listing" to "Booked Visit."

## 4. Technical & Trust Requirements
- **Mobile-First Design**: 70%+ of car buyers browse on mobile; the experience must be fluid.
- **SEO Optimization**: Unique URLs and metadata for every car listing to capture Google search traffic.
- **Secure Document Upload**: A portal for users to securely upload ID or finance documents once they move toward a purchase.
